# Yangwang

Yangwang is an ultra-premium electric vehicle brand under BYD Group, created to showcase the company’s most advanced technologies in performance, intelligence, and design. Launched in 2023, Yangwang — meaning “looking up” in Chinese — embodies ambition, innovation, and excellence, representing BYD’s push into the luxury EV segment on a global scale.
