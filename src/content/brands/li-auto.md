# Li Auto

Li Auto is a premium Chinese automotive brand dedicated to redefining family mobility through smart electric vehicles. Founded in 2015 under Li Auto Inc., the brand focuses on extended-range electric vehicles (EREVs) and pure EVs that balance innovation, practicality, and reliability—making it a top choice for tech-driven families in China’s fast-growing NEV market.
