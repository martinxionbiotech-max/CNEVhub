# Geely Galaxy

Geely Galaxy is a premium new-energy vehicle (NEV) brand under Geely Auto Group, created to showcase the company’s latest advancements in electrification, intelligence, and design. Launched in 2023, Geely Galaxy represents the group’s next-generation vision of green mobility, blending cutting-edge hybrid and pure electric technologies with futuristic aesthetics and digital innovation.
