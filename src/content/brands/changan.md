# ChangAn

Changan is the core consumer car brand of Chongqing Changan Automobile, known for combining dependable engineering with contemporary design and smart technology. Positioned for mass-market buyers, Changan focuses on making advanced features—connectivity, safety, and electrified powertrains—accessible without sacrificing value.
