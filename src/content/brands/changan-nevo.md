# ChangAn NEVO

Changan NEVO is the global new-energy vehicle brand under ChangAn Automobile, created to spearhead the company’s international electrification strategy. Officially launched in 2023, NEVO represents ChangAn’s vision of fusing intelligent technology, refined design, and sustainable innovation to deliver next-generation electric mobility for global users. The name “NEVO,” derived from “New Energy Vehicle Onward,” embodies progress, confidence, and the continuous pursuit of cleaner, smarter transportation.
