# Arcfox

Arcfox is a premium intelligent electric vehicle brand under BAIC Group, created to redefine the boundaries of performance, design, and digital innovation in the new-energy era. Officially launched in 2017 by BAIC BluePark New Energy Technology, Arcfox represents the group’s most advanced expression of electric mobility — blending cutting-edge engineering with Scandinavian-inspired aesthetics and Huawei-powered intelligence.
