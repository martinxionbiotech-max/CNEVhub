# Bestune

Bestune, formerly known as Besturn, is a mid-to-premium automotive brand under FAW Group Corporation, created to deliver intelligent, stylish, and high-value vehicles for a new generation of drivers. Established in 2006, Bestune represents FAW’s commitment to combining advanced technology, modern design, and refined performance within an accessible, user-centered brand.
