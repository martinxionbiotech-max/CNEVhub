# Dongfeng eπ

Dongfeng eπ (pronounced “e-pi”) is a new-energy vehicle brand under Dongfeng Motor Corporation, created to blend intelligent technology, performance, and youthful design into a new generation of electric mobility. Officially launched in 2023, eπ represents Dongfeng’s push to redefine mainstream electric vehicles with innovation that is both emotional and intelligent.
