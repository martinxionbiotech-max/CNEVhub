# Haval

Haval is the global SUV brand under Great Wall Motor, dedicated to creating intelligent, reliable, and stylish sport utility vehicles for modern drivers. Established in 2013, Haval has become one of China’s most recognized automotive brands, combining robust engineering with advanced technology to deliver vehicles that balance performance, comfort, and safety. The name “Haval,” derived from “Have All,” reflects the brand’s mission to offer a complete driving experience — power, intelligence, and versatility.

## Brand Overview
