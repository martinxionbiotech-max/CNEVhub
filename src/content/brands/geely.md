# Geely

Geely is one of China’s most influential automotive groups, known for its rapid global expansion and commitment to innovation. Founded in 1997, the company has grown from producing budget cars to owning premium brands like Volvo, Polestar, and Zeekr. Geely focuses on advanced electrification, smart connectivity, and user-centric design. With strong R&D and international manufacturing, Geely aims to shape the future of sustainable and intelligent mobility.
