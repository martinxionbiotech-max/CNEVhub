# AION

AION is the dedicated new-energy vehicle (NEV) brand under GAC Group, established in 2018 to lead the company’s electrification and intelligent mobility strategy. Positioned as a premium electric brand, AION combines advanced technology, futuristic design, and sustainable innovation to deliver high-performance electric vehicles that define the next era of smart transportation.
