# Hyptec

Hyptec, used to be Hyper before renamed in 2024, is a high-performance new-energy vehicle brand under GAC Aion, created to push the boundaries of intelligent electric mobility. Officially launched in 2022, Hyper represents the pinnacle of GAC’s technological innovation — combining cutting-edge engineering, futuristic design, and supercar-level performance to redefine what electric driving can be.
