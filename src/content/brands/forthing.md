# Forthing

Forthing, also known as Dongfeng Fengxing, is a passenger vehicle brand under Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of Dongfeng Motor Corporation. Established in 2001, Forthing focuses on creating practical, reliable, and intelligent vehicles for families and small businesses, combining Dongfeng’s solid engineering foundation with modern design and user-friendly innovation.
