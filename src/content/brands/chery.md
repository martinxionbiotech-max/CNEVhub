# Chery

Chery is the core passenger vehicle brand under Chery Automobile Co., Ltd., representing the company’s long-standing commitment to accessible innovation, reliability, and intelligent design. Since its debut in 1997, Chery has built a reputation as one of China’s most trusted automotive brands, known for producing vehicles that combine practicality, technology, and modern style for families and everyday drivers.
