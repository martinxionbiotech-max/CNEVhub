# Maextro

Maextro is a cutting-edge ultra-luxury electric vehicle brand launched in 2024 by JAC Group in partnership with Huawei, under the Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA) framework. Designed to compete at the highest echelon of EV luxury, Maextro blends JAC’s engineering and manufacturing strength with Huawei’s intelligent software, ADAS ecosystems, and smart cockpit technologies. Its name—derived from “Maestro” with an “X” to symbolize exploration—signals the brand’s ambition to redefine premium mobility through innovation and artistry. Wikipedia
